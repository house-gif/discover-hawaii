# AASEN Discover · Image guide

The app currently includes remote, illustrative Unsplash-hosted editorial images by category. These are not verified photographs of each individual listing. Card and detail screens disclose this distinction.

## Before commercial publication

- Check the current Unsplash license at https://unsplash.com/license and any restrictions applying to each image.
- Confirm each URL still works and retain creator attribution and provenance where possible.
- Replace generic images with verified, correctly identified location or business photography as rights are secured.
- For photos of private property, people, brands, or protected locations, assess whether permissions/releases are required for the proposed use.
- Consider downloading approved assets into the app for reliability and offline use.

The app does not yet include a fully licensed, location-specific image library.
